About

The classroom admin, in one place.

TeacherPal is a free web app for teachers. It covers attendance, seating, groups, bathroom passes and lesson plans.

What it does

Take attendance by clicking seats on your seating chart. Build your room and assign seats. Split the class into groups. Call on students with a name wheel. Log bathroom passes. Plan lessons by course on a weekly calendar. Put a timer or a noise meter on the projector.

It's built for two moments. On the projector, where everything has to be readable from the back row. And between classes, when you have a few minutes and every click counts: the right period is already open, because TeacherPal knows the bell schedule.

One roster, every tool

The idea underneath it: enter your students once. Every tool reads from the same roster, so adding a student puts them on the seating chart, in the groups, on the name wheel and in the pass tracker at the same time.

The tools share what happens during the day too. Mark a student absent and they're left out of today's groups and off the name wheel, with nothing else to update.
