        How to play

        Two teams try to guess the most popular answers to a survey question. Here is how a round goes.

        1. Team 1vsTeam 2
          Make two teams

          Split the class in two. Each team picks a name.

        2. We asked 100 students…Name something in your backpack
          Read the question

          Read it out loud. 100 students were asked it, and the board hides their most popular answers.

        3. Pencil30324
          Guess the answers

          A team calls out guesses. When a guess is on the board, click it and it flips over to show the answer and its points.

        4. ✕✕✕
          Three wrong guesses

          A wrong guess is a strike. After three strikes the other team gets one guess. If it's on the board, they steal the points.

        5. 64→Team 1
          Give the points

          The points from the round go to the team that won it. Then go on to the next question.

        6. Team 1 212Team 2 148
          Most points wins

          Play as many questions as you like. The team with the most points at the end wins.

        Good to know

        • On the keyboard: 1 to 8 flips an answer, X gives a strike, and the arrow keys change the question.
        • Using a projector? Press the full screen window button and drag that window to the projector. Your laptop then shows every answer, so only you can see them.
        • Made a mistake? Undo takes back the last thing you did.
