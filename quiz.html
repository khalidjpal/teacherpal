How to play

Teams pick a category and an amount, then answer the clue to win the points. Here is how it goes.

  1. Team 1Team 2Team 3
    Make teams

    Two to four teams. Each team picks a name.

  2. 100100100200200200300300300
    Pick a square

    A team chooses a category and an amount. Click that square. Bigger amounts are harder clues.

  3. He kills Duncan.
    Read the clue

    Read it out loud and let the team answer. Press Space to show the right answer.

  4. +300−300
    Right or wrong

    Right: click + to give the team the points. Wrong: click − to take them away. Scores can go below zero.

  5. Double!
    Hidden doubles

    A few squares hide a Double. The team bets some of its points before it sees the clue.

  6. Team 2 1,800Team 1 1,200
    Most points wins

    Round 2 doubles every amount. When the board is done, the team with the most points wins.

Good to know

  • Every square goes dark once it has been played. Click it again to fix a score.
  • Using a projector? Press the full screen window button and drag that window to the projector. Your laptop then shows every clue with its answer, so only you can see them.
  • Made a mistake? Undo takes back the last thing you did.
